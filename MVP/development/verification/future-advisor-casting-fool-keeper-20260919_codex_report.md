- Candidate direction: early-to-mid thirties.
- Function remains mature dry humor, emotional distance and resilience after hardship.
- The character understands the wound and the room before using humor; never mocks vulnerability.
- Visual cues: off-center informal seat, repaired charcoal/plum clothing, plain wooden game piece, restrained knowing expression.

## Keeper of First Intent (working title)

- New inner-function proposal, separate from the Fool and Chaplain.
- Candidate direction: female royal apprentice, approximately 16–17.
- Core function: recover the first honest desire or simple perception before fear, status and strategy accumulate.
- Strength: asks clear, uncomplicated questions when the council has become self-perpetuating.
- Excess risk: may underweight responsibility, history and real danger.
- Suggested unlock: after repeated strategies obscure why the king began pursuing the issue.
- Visual cues: undyed cream/gray clothing, muted sky-blue accent, smooth unmarked river stone, open attentive gaze.

## Boundaries

- Youth does not mean ignorance, comedy mascot behavior or romantic framing.
- Purity does not mean moral superiority or always-correct advice.
- Neither candidate is named or approved for story insertion yet.
