## 1. 테사리엔 · 개혁가 — Reformer

Name status: **provisional**

### Differentiation target

- Apparent age: mid-40s
- Presentation: masculine or androgynous
- Build: tall, lean, long-limbed
- Face: long angular face, high cheekbones, slightly crooked nose, alert deep-set eyes
- Hair: short dark curls with visible gray at the temples; irregular but maintained
- Accent: muted oxidized green with worn brick-red inner layer
- Silhouette: long diagonal outer layer with one sleeve deliberately rolled higher than the other
- Gesture/prop: a thick practical cord rests across a waist-height wooden rail; one hand loosens an old knot while the other holds the freed straight length. Both cord and hands are physically supported and fully visible.

### Generation prompt

> A tall, lean court reformer in their mid-40s, long angular face, high cheekbones, slightly crooked nose, deep-set attentive eyes, short dark curls graying at the temples. Their asymmetrical but practical layered coat uses muted oxidized green and worn brick-red accents. At a waist-height plain wooden rail, they calmly loosen one old knot in a thick utility cord while the other hand holds the newly freed straight section. The gesture suggests examining an inherited method, not rebellion or destruction. Upright forward-moving posture, controlled intensity, restrained expression, no smile of triumph. The rail supports the cord naturally. No books, papers, plans, broken chains, revolutionary banner, weapon, or magical symbol.

### Validation

- Reads as changing a method, rather than destroying tradition for spectacle.
- Knot and cord are supported and physically plausible.
- Does not resemble Seran's document work or Valen's action posture.

Name status: **confirmed**

### Differentiation target

- Presentation: masculine
- Build: lean, straight-backed, narrow shoulders
- Face: narrow rectangular face, heavy lower eyelids, prominent nose bridge, short gray hair

- Silhouette: severe vertical layers with no ceremonial ornament

> A lean royal judge in his late 50s, straight-backed with narrow shoulders, narrow rectangular face, heavy lower eyelids, prominent nose bridge, short gray hair. Severe but unornamented vertical court layers in dark muted burgundy and pale stone-gray trim. No object in his hands. One relaxed open hand is held near his chest while the other is extended a measured distance away, quietly separating two scopes of responsibility. No scales, gavel, law book, sword, courtroom emblem, pointing finger, clenched fist, or execution imagery.

### Validation

- Hands remain relaxed and fully inside frame.

## 4. 미레시아 · 의관 — Physician

Name status: **provisional**

### Differentiation target

- Apparent age: late 60s
- Presentation: feminine
- Build: medium, softly rounded, grounded stance
- Face: soft triangular face, fine age lines, slightly hooded eyes, calm asymmetrical mouth
- Hair: short silver curls, uncovered
- Accent: faded teal-gray and warm undyed linen
- Silhouette: rounded shawl-like outer layer over a practical tunic; no robe of office
- Gesture/prop: a shallow wooden tray rests on a low table, carrying a plain cup of water and folded clean cloth; one hand checks the water temperature while the other steadies the tray.

### Generation prompt

> A grounded royal physician in her late 60s, medium softly rounded build, soft triangular face, fine age lines, slightly hooded observant eyes, short silver curls left uncovered. Faded teal-gray and warm undyed-linen practical layers with a rounded outer silhouette. A shallow wooden tray rests fully on a low plain table, holding a simple cup of water and one folded clean cloth. One hand lightly checks the water temperature while the other steadies the tray. Her attention is calm and practical, assessing whether someone can continue rather than promising a cure. No book, herb bundle, potion, syringe, caduceus, medical cross, magical healing light, plague mask, or distressed patient.

### Validation

- Reads as checking sustainability and recovery, not solving every problem medically.
- Tray, water, cloth, and hands are supported and anatomically plausible.
- Age and rounded silhouette distinguish her from Ardet and Orban.
