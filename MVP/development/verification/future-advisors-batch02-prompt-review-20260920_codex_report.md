# Future advisors batch 02 prompt review — Codex report

## User-approved scope

Prepare portrait-generation prompts for the four future advisors that still lack approved visual sources: Reformer Tesarien, Architect Dovan, Judge Dan, and Physician Miresia. Do not generate or integrate portraits before prompt review.

## Design result

The prompts separate the characters through age, body shape, face geometry, posture, accent color, and grounded hand action:

- Tesarien: mid-40s, tall and angular, loosening a supported utility knot.
- Dovan: early 30s, short and sturdy, testing a supported wooden joint model.
- Dan: late 50s, lean and vertical, using empty hands to mark separate scopes of responsibility.
- Miresia: late 60s, softly rounded, checking water and cloth on a supported tray.

Books and documents are excluded. Role clichés such as scales, gavels, blueprints, medical symbols, and magical healing are also excluded. Each prompt requires full hands and props inside the frame and preserves non-canon status.

## Rian mockup review

The large portrait, letter, open-hand gesture, speaker token, and exact dialogue make Rian's diplomatic function more memorable. The line reports an observable action and does not state the other party's hidden feelings. Before runtime use, replace or remove the large background banner emblems because they can be mistaken for canonical heraldry. The mockup remains a non-canon layout reference.
